01 / THE TEAM

More than a robot.
A community in motion.

We are FTC Team 23892 Hivemind, based in the Charlotte, North Carolina area and part of the Queen City Robotics Alliance (QCRA).

We’re a second-year team with a growing ambition: to design, build, and program robots while helping more people discover the excitement of STEM. We were originally known as Artificial Unintelligence — a name that reflects the humor and curiosity we bring to the work.

For us, robotics is not just about what we can build. It’s about what we can learn together, who we can help, and how far a shared idea can travel.
